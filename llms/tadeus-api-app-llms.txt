# Tadeus - AI-Powered Qualitative Research

Scale your qualitative insights with AI agents that interview, moderate, and synthesize human sentiment in real-time.

## Key Features
- **AI Interviewers**: Natural voice-based AI agents that conduct human-like interviews.
- **Real-time Synthesis**: Instant analysis and summarization of qualitative data.
- **Campaign Management**: Tools for researchers to design and deploy surveys at scale.
- **Privacy First**: Secure, anonymized handling of research participants' data.
